Software Engineer

I build software across web, mobile, backend, computer vision and hardware.

I like working with people on small, useful tools that stay useful. Paid cloud infrastructure isn’t always necessary: there are often overlooked ways to run an app at zero cost. Where I can, I build for the browser and use free services for storage, keeping maintenance low so projects can survive beyond the initial enthusiasm.

When you commission a project, you get full ownership of the finished product, with a clean handover once the work is complete. You can come back for improvements, further iterations or fixes. I’m not currently offering ongoing project management.
